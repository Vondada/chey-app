# DM / text / IG pitch scripts — local businesses

Tone: short, human, no hype guarantees. Swap `[brackets]`. Send only to people you know or who know of you.

---

## 1) Warm text / SMS (best for tonight)

```
Hey [Name] — quick one. I can turn out same-night ad creatives for [Business]: 6 IG-ready images + 2 captions for $500 (or a smaller $150/$250 pack). You send logo + a few product pics + the offer. I deliver tonight after payment. Want me to hold a slot?
```

If they ask what you need:

```
Logo (PNG if you have it), 2–6 photos of [product/space], and the exact offer (price + CTA). Cash App / Venmo / card link — your pick. Once those land I start.
```

---

## 2) Instagram DM (slightly warmer)

```
Hi [Name]! Love what [Business] is posting. I make same-night ad creatives for local shops — Tonight Pack is 6 posts/stories + 2 captions for $500. Smaller packs at $150/$250. You send logo + pics + offer; I send PNGs ready to post. Open to a quick trial tonight?
```

Follow-up if they go quiet (one bump only, ~4–6 hours later):

```
No pressure — if timing’s off I can do a 2-image starter for $150 instead. Either way, hope [weekend/promo] goes well.
```

---

## 3) Facebook Messenger / Google Business message

```
Hi — I’m [Your name]. I help local businesses with same-night social ads: images + captions, no ad-account setup. Packages $150 / $250 / $500 tonight. If you send logo + photos + your offer, I can deliver before [time]. Interested?
```

---

## 4) In-person / after you buy something (30-second pitch)

```
I make AI ad creatives for places like this — same night. Six posts for five hundred, or a cheaper starter. If you want, text me your logo and tonight’s special and I’ll send samples before close.
```

Leave a card or a text with your Cash App handle.

---

## 5) Reply when they say “send examples”

```
Here’s a sample style board from a similar [salon/cafe/gym] (no client secrets). For you I’d match [your colors] and put [offer] front and center. Want the $250 mid pack or the full $500 Tonight Pack?
```

Attach 2–3 of your own portfolio mockups (generic local business — not cannabis branding, not stolen competitor ads).

---

## 6) Closing / payment

```
Great — I’ll send the checklist. Pay via Cash App $YOUR_CASHAPP or Venmo @YOUR_VENMO (or Stripe link). I start when payment + logo/photos/offer are in. Target delivery: [time tonight].
```

---

## What not to say

- “Guaranteed $500 sales / viral / ROI”
- “I already set up your Meta ads” (unless they hired you for that separately)
- Anything implying CHE/Stripe is already earning for them without keys connected
- Overpromising video tonight (this pack is stills + captions)
