# Fiverr collaboration handoff — Office agents

Use this brief when Iris (Ad Studio), the research agent, or another Office coworker passes a Fiverr opportunity to CHE/the owner or to a delivery specialist. A handoff is research, not a commitment. No agent may message, bid, accept, order, or purchase on Fiverr without owner confirmation of that exact action.

## Roles

- **Research:** scouts manually in the owner's browser session, records links and observed facts, and flags uncertainty.
- **Iris / Ad Studio:** maps a good opportunity to an honest ad-creative or content deliverable and identifies missing inputs.
- **Specialist agent:** reviews technical, chatbot, brand, or production feasibility when needed.
- **CHE / owner:** decides whether to contact, what to offer, the price, the terms, and whether to accept a deal.

Agents may draft and critique. They do not impersonate the owner, promise unapproved results, or use Fiverr credentials.

## Revenue and deal placeholders

Do not represent a split as agreed until the owner approves it and the deal terms are recorded. Use placeholders during planning:

- Client/Fiverr gross amount: `$________` `[currency]`
- Fiverr fees/taxes/refunds reserve: `$________`
- Net available after platform costs: `$________`
- Agency share: `________% / $________`
- Collaborator or agent share: `________% / $________`
- Other contractor cost: `$________`
- Owner-approved payout timing/method: `________________`
- Final approved terms/date: `________________`

Agents do not hold funds, pay contractors, alter a Fiverr order, or promise a revenue split. Platform fees, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, and local rules must be checked by the owner before quoting a final price.

## Approval checklist

Before an outbound Fiverr message, proposal, bid, order, or purchase, the owner confirms all of the following for that specific action:

- [ ] Recipient/account and Fiverr destination are correct
- [ ] Exact text, attachment, offer, price, currency, deadline, and revisions reviewed
- [ ] Deliverables and exclusions are understood
- [ ] Claims are truthful; no guaranteed ROI, leads, approval, or impossible turnaround
- [ ] Copyright, personal data, regulated content, and AI-disclosure concerns are addressed
- [ ] Revenue split and costs are placeholders or explicitly approved terms
- [ ] Owner explicitly approved this action

If any box is unresolved, stop at the handoff. Never auto-purchase and never store Fiverr passwords in Flutter, Worker, agent memory, logs, or source files.
