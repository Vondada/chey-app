# Tonight Pack — AI Ad Creatives (same-night)

Honest framing: this pack is designed so you can **invoice ~$500 tonight** if you already have local business contacts who will reply and pay. It is **not** a guarantee. Cold outreach with no prior relationship often converts slowly. Start with people who already know you (past clients, friends who own shops, nearby restaurants/salons/gyms you visit).

## What the client must send (before work starts)

Use [ORDER_CHECKLIST.md](./ORDER_CHECKLIST.md). Minimum:

1. **Logo** (PNG with transparent background preferred; SVG/JPG ok)
2. **2–6 product / space photos** (phone photos fine; well-lit)
3. **Offer text** (what is the deal? price? deadline? CTA: call / book / DM / visit)
4. **Business name**, city, phone or booking link, brand colors if they have them
5. **Where it will post** (IG, Facebook, Google, flyer print — affects aspect ratio)

No assets → no start clock.
